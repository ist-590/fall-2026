## Generative AI and Human Subjects Research

* Unique ethical and regulatory concerns
* Risks related to privacy, consent, and secondary use of data
* Do not input identifiable or “restricted” data unless [**explicitly approved**]{.t-salmon} by IRB and data agreements

::: {.t-ref}
[Generative AI in Research](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/education/generative-ai-in-research/)
:::

---


### Need for Informed Consent
* Participants must be informed if generative AI may be used to process or analyze their data

::: {.t-ref}
[Generative AI in Research](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/education/generative-ai-in-research/)
:::

---

### Consent language should cover
  * Whether AI tools will be used during or after the study
  * Risks of reidentification from metadata or advanced computational methods
  * How long data will be stored and whether future AI-based analyses are anticipated

::: {.t-ref}
[Generative AI in Research](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/education/generative-ai-in-research/)
:::

---

### Use of AI After Study Completion

* AI for future, undetermined research requires explicit participant consent or additional IRB approval

::: {.t-ref}
[Generative AI in Research](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/education/generative-ai-in-research/)
:::

---



### Privacy and deidentification risks
* Even when direct identifiers are removed, AI tools may process or infer personal information
  * e.g., residual data, patterns, or linked metadata
* Metadata can inadvertently disclose sensitive information
  * e.g., file names
  * Remove metadata before using generative AI


::: {.t-ref}
[Generative AI in Research](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/education/generative-ai-in-research/)
:::

---

### Best Practices for Generative AI and Human Subjects Research
* Document all AI uses in data management plans and IRB protocols
* When in doubt, consult the IRB or research compliance office before applying AI tools to human subjects data

::: {.t-ref}
[Generative AI in Research](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/education/generative-ai-in-research/)
:::
