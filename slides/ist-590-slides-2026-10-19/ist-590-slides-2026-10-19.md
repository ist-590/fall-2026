### Predatory publishing

* Exploits authors
* Can lead to reputational harm


::: {.t-ref}
[Penn State Guidelines](https://researchsupport.psu.edu/orp/research-integrity/predatory-publishing/)
:::

---

#### Key differences between reputable and predatory venues
  * Adequate, qualified review
  * Qualified, independent editorial oversight
  * Transparent fees, procedures, and policies
  * Acceptance by reputable scholarly organizations

::: {.t-ref}
[ThinkSCIENCE](https://thinkscience.co.jp/en/articles/predatory-journals)
:::
