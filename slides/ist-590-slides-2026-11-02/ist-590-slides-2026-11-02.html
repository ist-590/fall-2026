Critical to explore different career paths

  • Internships
  • Career-focused workshops
    • Conferences
    • Penn State
  • Alumni panels

Build relationships

  • What are people with training similar to you doing?
  • What are the opportunities beyond academia?

Attend job talks in IST

  • My recommendation: Regularly attend job talks in your department

Attend job talks in IST

  • How do the candidates frame their contributions?

  • What questions do they get from the audience?
    • How do they handle QAs?

Meet with job candidates

  • Why did they decide to pursue academic careers?
  • What suggestions do they have for you?
