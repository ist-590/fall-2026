### Only 20–25% of PhDs secure tenure-track positions

* The number varies widely across different disciplines

::: {.t-ref}
[PhD graduates pursuing careers beyond academia: A scoping review](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2025.2515211)
:::

---



### "Career intentions often change during doctoral study"

* Students often start PhDs with an academic career plan
* But later, they consider broader options

::: {.t-ref}
[Beyond Academia Is Not a Backup Plan: What the Research Says About PhD Careers](https://grad.rutgers.edu/news/what-research-says-about-phd-careers)
:::

---


### Start thinking about your career development [early]{.t-clover}
* [**Don't wait**]{.t-salmon} till your final year

---

### Critical to explore different career paths

* What's available?
* What do [**you**]{.t-clover} want to do?

---

### Engage in "structured exposure"
* Internships
* Career-focused workshops
  * Conferences
  * Penn State
* Alumni panels

---

### Build relationships

* What are people with training similar to you doing?
* What are the opportunities beyond academia?

---



### Attend job talks in IST
* Explore the norms of academic careers
* My recommendation: [**Regularly attend**]{.t-salmon} job talks in your department

---

### Attend job talks in IST

* How do the candidates frame their contributions?
  * Read their CVs and research statements
* What questions do they get from the audience?
  * How do they handle QAs?



---




### Meet with job candidates

* Ask them about their journey
* Why did they decide to pursue academic careers?
* What suggestions do they have for you?
