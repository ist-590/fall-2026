Final Grade Policy

  • A: 100–93%
  • A-: 92–90%
  • B+: 89–87%
  • B: 86–83%
  • B-: 82–80%
  • C+: 79–77%
  • C: 76–70%
  • D: 69–60%
  • F: <60%

The cutoff point for each grade is the lower number without rounding (e.g., 89.9% will result in B+)
